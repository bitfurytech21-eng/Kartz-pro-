Privacy Policy

Application Domain: https://kretz.site • Effective: January 1, 2025

Kretz Real Estate ("we", "our", or "the Agency") operates the web application accessible at https://kretz.site. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our prospective buyers, property owners, and authenticated clients.

1. Data We Collect

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In or create an account with your email, we collect only the necessary credentials required to establish and maintain your user session:

  • Account Identification: Full Name, Email Address, and Avatar image URL provided by Google Identity Services.
  • User Saved Preferences: Property listings saved to your private favorites, viewing appointments scheduled via Google Calendar, and search criteria alerts.

2. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Our application strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request standard, non-sensitive identity scopes (openid, .../auth/userinfo.email, and .../auth/userinfo.profile).
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not transfer user data to advertising networks, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do not read or inspect your private email communications.

3. Data Storage & Encryption

All transmitted information is secured using TLS 1.3 encryption. User databases and authentication tokens are hosted within Google Cloud / Firebase enterprise data centers with AES-256 encryption at rest.
